# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (bozza). Will become a custom WordPress theme, no page builder. Shared styles in `assets/css/site.css`, shared behavior in `assets/js/site.js`.

## Users

Owners and technicians of companies working in foundations, slope consolidation, micropiles/anchors, geothermal and stone quarries. They evaluate a machine to buy or rent and compare technical data before asking for a quote.

## Product Purpose

Website of Ripamonti Dr. Gianni Srl (Ornavasso, VB, since 1934): drilling rigs and equipment for rock, foundations and quarries. On product pages success means a quote/information request; downloading the PDF datasheet is secondary.

## Positioning

Italian manufacturer born in quarries (1934), designing and building its own rigs in Ornavasso, also distributor of major drilling brands.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Languages IT and EN (EN visible but inactive in the draft).
- 8 product lines (see index.html).
- Technical data only from the official PDF datasheets in `assets/doc/`; old website data is no longer valid.
- 3D model viewer (model-viewer, local) is a paid add-on; Eagle 1025 is a demonstration.
- Responsive to 360 px, visible keyboard focus, prefers-reduced-motion respected. Draft is noindex.

## Brand Commitments

- Official colors only: RAL 7001 (#8F999F), RAL 7021 (#2F3234), white, black. Flag green only for tiny details, client prefers none.
- Fonts: Barlow Condensed (headings), Barlow (text), self-hosted.
- Slanted-cut buttons and labels from the brochures.
- On product pages, the 4 key datasheet figures with the brochure icons.
- Short, clear, correct Italian. Copy written by us must be flagged for client validation.

## Evidence on Hand

Photos supplied by Ripamonti in `assets/img/`, datasheet `assets/doc/Eagle-1025-scheda-tecnica.pdf`, 3D model `assets/model/eagle-1025.glb`. No testimonials, case studies or prices: do not invent them.

## Product Principles

- Technical truth first: every number traceable to the datasheet.
- Easy to understand at a glance for people on a job site.
- Modern, clean, technical; reference Comacchio only as inspiration.
